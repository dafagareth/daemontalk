# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 2026**

Daemontalk operates under a strict zero-tracker policy. Your reading activity and personal privacy are fully respected.

---

## 1. Zero Tracking & No Third-Party Ads

This site does not use Google Analytics, advertising networks, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral trackers. All static assets, fonts, and scripts are delivered directly from our own server rather than external tracking CDNs.

---

## 2. Client-Side Storage & Preferences

We use standard browser localStorage exclusively to persist your reading preferences on your local device. This includes saved bookmarks in your reading list, dark or light theme selection, and typography preference (serif or sans-serif). This data remains strictly on your device and is never transmitted to our server or any third party.

---

## 3. Server Logs & Security

When accessing the site, standard HTTP connection metadata (such as IP address, User-Agent, and requested URL) is logged temporarily for rate limiting and server security auditing. These logs are automatically rotated and deleted after 14 days, and are never shared, sold, or used for user profiling.

---

## 4. Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy practices on this site, feel free to reach out via email at: **realdaemontalk@gmail.com**.
